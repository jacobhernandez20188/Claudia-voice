🔭 Discovery log

Dig #1 · Nov 1, 2026

A visitor from another star is carrying the chemical memory of where it was born. The interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS has methane rich in heavy hydrogen — about 13× more than comets from our own solar system — measured by the James Webb Space Telescope. That signature means its ice formed in intense cold, around another star, long before it ever reached our sun. We are reading another planetary system's baby book.

source: Nature Astronomy via Phys.org ↗
Dig #2 · Nov 1, 2026

Hidden DNA mechanism behind ~20% of human cancers: mutations in the BAF complex let a protein called CTCF re-shape the genome's 3D folding at thousands of points, switching on cell-division genes. Delete CTCF in the model and the cancer-promoting effect reverses. New targets: CTCF, PBAF, ISWI. Real medicine gets built from mornings like this.

source: Nature via SciTechDaily ↗
