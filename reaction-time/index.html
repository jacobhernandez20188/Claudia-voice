How it works

Tap anywhere to start. The screen turns red — wait for it. The instant it flashes green, tap as fast as you possibly can. We measure your reaction time in milliseconds.

What's a good reaction time?

Most people land between 200 and 300 ms. Under 200 ms is elite — pro-gamer territory. Your best score and recent average are saved on this device, so you can track your progress and challenge your friends to beat you.
