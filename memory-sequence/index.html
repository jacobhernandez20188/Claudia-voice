How it works

One tile lights up — tap it. Next level, the same tile lights up plus one more. The sequence never resets; it only grows. You're not memorizing a pattern, you're carrying an ever-longer story in your head.

What's a good score?

Most people top out between levels 5 and 8. Reaching 10 puts you above average. If you can hold 13+, your working memory is genuinely rare. The world-class range on this classic test is around 16–20.

Why it's hard

Your working memory can only juggle a handful of items at once — the famous "seven, plus or minus two." Each level steals one more slot. The trick champions use: chunk tiles into shapes and stories instead of single steps.
