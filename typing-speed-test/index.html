How it works

Tap the passage above and start typing — the clock starts on your first keystroke. We measure your speed in words per minute (WPM) and your accuracy. Green letters are correct, red are mistakes. Finish the text to lock in your score.

What's a good typing speed?

Average is around 40 WPM. Over 60 is fast, over 80 is pro territory — and on a phone keyboard, anything over 50 is genuinely impressive. Your best score is saved on this device.
